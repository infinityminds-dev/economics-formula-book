CURRENT LESSON

Arithmetic Mean — Individual Series (Direct Method)

X̄ = ΣX ÷ N

1. Understand the symbols

X̄
Arithmetic Mean — the average value.
Σ
Summation — add all the values.
X
Each observation or data value.
N
Total number of observations.

2. What does the formula do?

First add every observation to get ΣX. Then count how many observations there are to get N. Finally divide the total by the count. This gives the arithmetic mean.

3. Choose difficulty

Small values and a direct calculation.

Quick formula revision

X̄ = ΣX ÷ N

ΣX = sum of all observations. N = number of observations. Divide the sum by the number of observations to find the mean.

Rounding-off: This example gives an exact whole-number answer, so no rounding is needed. If a question asks for a specified number of decimal places, follow that instruction and explain the rounding rule before rounding.
